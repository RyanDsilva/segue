Each song's time comes from its tempo and the bars you play: Segue counts the bars of the chart (one bar per chord unless the chart or your [chord timing](/guide/chord-timing) says otherwise) at the song's tempo. An [arranged](/guide/arrange-sections) slot counts only the passes it plays, so a skipped section adds nothing and a chorus played twice counts twice.

The **Time** column shows each song's time, for example "3:40". Hover over it to see the tempo and the number of bars. A time that rests on Segue's default of one bar per chord reads "~3:40": correct the chord lengths in [Chord timing](/guide/chord-timing) to make it exact. The builder adds the times into a running time for each part and the whole set, next to the setlist's target if it has one.

## Breaks {#breaks}

Choose **Setlist > Insert Break** (<kbd>⇧⌘B</kbd>) to split the set into parts, such as Set 1 and Set 2. Each part shows its own total. On stage, a break shows which song the next part starts with; press <kbd>→</kbd> to carry on. The show clock keeps running.

## Break length {#break-length}

Choose **Edit Break…** on a break row to give it a length. It counts in the whole setlist's total only.

## Changeover {#changeover}

Changeover is the time between songs: tuning, a sip of water, a word to the crowd. Set it for a setlist under **Details…**, or set the default in **Settings > General**. It is added after every song but the last of each part.

A [medley](/guide/medleys) counts as one row: its time is its songs' times added together, with no changeover between them and one after it. If one of its songs has no time, the medley's time reads "≥5:30" and the song is listed with the others below.

## Songs with no time {#unknown}

Segue never guesses a time. A song has none when it has no tempo, or no chords to count. Its row reads "--:--", it adds nothing, later start times become "at least" times like "≥13:50", and the total says how many are unknown, for example "≥38:20 + 2 unknown", so it never looks more precise than it is.

Click "unknown" to list exactly which songs have no time. For a song with no tempo, type one in its row, or click **Tap** and tap along, then press <kbd>Return</kbd>: Segue writes the tempo into the song and every total updates. A song with no chords says "No chords to time"; click **Open Song** to add them.
