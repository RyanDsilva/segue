<Steps>
1. Open the setlist and choose **Setlist > Start Show** (<kbd>⌘↩</kbd>).
2. Check the short list under **Before you start**, then click **Start Show**.
3. Play. <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> is the next song, and <kbd>?</kbd> shows every key.
</Steps>

## Before you start {#checklist}

The checklist says whether the charger is connected and reminds you to turn the screen brightness up. If you've set up [Gig Mode Shortcuts](/guide/gig-mode), a Focus row ticks once your Shortcut has run, and the [metronome](/guide/metronome) row turns its pulse on or off for this show. Hide any row for this show, or for good.

## Move through the set {#navigate}

<kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> goes to the next song and <kbd>←</kbd> to the previous one. <kbd>Page Down</kbd> scrolls down through the song, and at the end of the song the next press moves on to the next song; <kbd>Page Up</kbd> does the same backwards. So a page-turner pedal runs the whole set with no setup. At the end of the last song <kbd>Page Down</kbd> stays put and shows "Last song"; <kbd>→</kbd> ends the set. A double tap never skips a song, and holding a key down only keeps scrolling or zooming: it never changes songs or flips a setting. <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>↑</kbd> scroll a page within the song only, and <kbd>Home</kbd> goes back to the top. With the section map, <kbd>Page Down</kbd> still turns one screenful at a time and then moves to the next song.

## The section map {#section-map}

The stage writes out every section and every repeat in full, in the order you'll play them. The strip under the title is the song's roadmap: one chip per section, sized by its length and named like "Chorus 2/3" when a section comes round more than once. The section you're in is filled and has a coral bar along the bottom that fills as you go; sections you've played are dimmed. In the song itself the current section has a coral dot by its name, and a repeated section carries a "2 of 3" badge under its name. If your [arrangement](/guide/arrange-sections) shows a section as chords only or its first line only, its chip and its label say CHORDS or 1ST LINE.

## Jump to a section {#jump}

Click a chip, or press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> for the first nine sections. The song scrolls to that section (auto-scroll keeps going if it was on) and a note says where you landed, like "Jumped to Chorus · 2 of 3". Hover over a chip to see its number key.

## Fit and the minimum lyric size {#minimum-size}

Segue fits each song to the screen. Set the smallest size you can read in **Settings > Live**, under **Minimum lyric size**. A song that doesn't fit at that size scrolls instead of shrinking.

## Auto-scroll {#auto-scroll}

Press <kbd>A</kbd> to start or pause auto-scroll, <kbd>[</kbd> to slow it down and <kbd>]</kbd> to speed it up. The speed follows the song's tempo and the bars you play, so the song reaches the bottom as you finish it. A song with no tempo starts at a default speed, and the stage suggests adding a tempo.

## Columns and zoom {#view}

<kbd>C</kbd> cycles a song between automatic, one, two and three columns, and Segue remembers it for that song. <kbd>⌘+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘-</kbd> zoom, <kbd>⌘0</kbd> returns to the actual size.

## Playing bass {#bass}

Press <kbd>B</kbd> to switch between chords and the bass note to play, at sounding pitch. The header shows the key's scale, and a mini neck at the bottom left shows the chord you're reading, with the next walk-up as numbered notes. See [Playing bass](/guide/play-bass).

## Singer view {#singer-view}

Press <kbd>V</kbd> to hide every chord and show the lyrics larger, for a singer reading your screen; press it again for the chords. The stage stays on the same section. A [Singer Screen](/guide/singer-view#singer-screen) can show it on a second display while you keep your chords. See [Singer view](/guide/singer-view).

## Breaks {#breaks}

At a break in the setlist, the stage shows which song the next part starts with. Press <kbd>→</kbd> to carry on.

## Skip and reorder {#skip}

Press <kbd>X</kbd> to skip a song and <kbd>S</kbd> to see the set order, where you can drag songs and bring a skipped one back.

## End the show {#end}

Press <kbd>⌘.</kbd>, then <kbd>Return</kbd> to end or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep playing. The show goes into your [gig log](/guide/gig-log).

## If Segue quits mid-show {#resume}

If your Mac restarts or Segue quits during a show, Segue offers to resume it the next time it opens, at the same song and scroll position.
