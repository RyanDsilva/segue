<Steps>
1. Open the setlist and choose **Setlist > Start Show** (<kbd>⌘↩</kbd>).
2. Check the short list under **Before you start**, then click **Start Show**.
3. Play. <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> is the next song, and <kbd>?</kbd> shows every key.
</Steps>

## Before you start {#checklist}

The checklist says whether the charger is connected and reminds you to turn the screen brightness up. If you've set up [Gig Mode Shortcuts](/guide/gig-mode), a Focus row ticks once your Shortcut has run, and the [metronome](/guide/metronome) row turns its pulse on or off for this show. Hide any row for this show, or for good.

## Move through the set {#navigate}

<kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> goes to the next song and <kbd>←</kbd> to the previous one. <kbd>Page Down</kbd> scrolls down through the song, and at the end of the song the next press moves on to the next song; <kbd>Page Up</kbd> does the same backwards. So a page-turner pedal runs the whole set with no setup. <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>↑</kbd> scroll a page within the song only, and <kbd>Home</kbd> goes back to the top.

## Fit and the minimum lyric size {#minimum-size}

Segue fits each song to the screen. Set the smallest size you can read in **Settings > Live**, under **Minimum lyric size**. A song that doesn't fit at that size scrolls instead of shrinking.

## Auto-scroll {#auto-scroll}

Press <kbd>A</kbd> to start or pause auto-scroll, <kbd>[</kbd> to slow it down and <kbd>]</kbd> to speed it up.

## Columns and zoom {#view}

<kbd>C</kbd> cycles a song between automatic, one, two and three columns, and Segue remembers it for that song. <kbd>⌘+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘-</kbd> zoom, <kbd>⌘0</kbd> returns to the actual size.

## Breaks {#breaks}

At a break in the setlist, the stage shows which song the next part starts with. Press <kbd>→</kbd> to carry on.

<Shot id="stage-break" alt="The break screen: Break, Set 2 starts with Kettle On, Lights Low" />

## Skip and reorder {#skip}

Press <kbd>X</kbd> to skip a song and <kbd>S</kbd> to see the set order, where you can drag songs and bring a skipped one back.

## End the show {#end}

Press <kbd>⌘.</kbd>, then <kbd>Return</kbd> to end or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep playing. The show goes into your [gig log](/guide/gig-log).

## If Segue quits mid-show {#resume}

If your Mac restarts or Segue quits during a show, Segue offers to resume it the next time it opens, at the same song and scroll position.
