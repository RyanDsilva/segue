The first time you open Segue, it asks two separate questions: **Share usage analytics** and **Send crash reports**. Nothing is picked for you, and Segue has no account and never uploads your songs.

Change either answer any time in **Settings > Privacy**.

<Shot id="settings-privacy" alt="Settings, Privacy, with the two switches" />

The [privacy policy](/privacy) says exactly what each one sends, and to whom.
