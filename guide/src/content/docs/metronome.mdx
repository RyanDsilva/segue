## The pulse {#pulse}

At the bottom right of the stage, above the footer, the tempo sits beside one large pip per beat of the bar: four in 4/4, three in 3/4. Each pip flashes on its beat, and the first beat of each bar is coral. With Reduce Motion on, a number counts the beats instead (also for bars of more than eight beats). Press <kbd>M</kbd> to show or hide it for this show, or use the Metronome row in the pre-show checklist. To start every show with it on, choose **Settings > Live > Start shows with the metronome on**.

## Songs with no tempo {#no-tempo}

The metronome hides for a song without a tempo. Add one on the [song card](/guide/song-card#tempo).

## Compound meters {#meters}

In 6/8 and other compound meters, the pulse follows the dotted quarter: two pulses per bar in 6/8.

## The loop count-in {#count-in}

When a [rehearsal loop](/guide/rehearse#loop) goes back to the top of its section, the pips show for one bar to count you in, even if the pulse is off. The click sounds too if it's on. A song without a tempo just pauses for two seconds.

## The click, in rehearsals only {#click}

In a rehearsal, press <kbd>K</kbd> to hear a click on each beat. It starts off every time, and there is never a sound during a show.
