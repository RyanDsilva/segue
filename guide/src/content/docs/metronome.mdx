## The pulse {#pulse}

A small dot in the bottom right of the stage pulses at the song's tempo and time signature, with the first beat of each bar in coral. Press <kbd>M</kbd> to show or hide it for this show, or use the Metronome row in the pre-show checklist. To start every show with it on, choose **Settings > Live > Start shows with the metronome on**.

<Shot id="metronome" alt="The metronome pulse and tempo in the corner of the stage" />

## Songs with no tempo {#no-tempo}

The metronome hides for a song without a tempo. Add one on the [song card](/guide/song-card#tempo).

## Compound meters {#meters}

In 6/8 and other compound meters, the pulse follows the dotted quarter: two pulses per bar in 6/8.

## The click, in rehearsals only {#click}

In a rehearsal, press <kbd>K</kbd> to hear a click on each beat. It starts off every time, and there is never a sound during a show.
