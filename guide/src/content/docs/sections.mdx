In Visual mode, the section band runs down the left of the lyrics, one bar per section.

<Shot id="band" alt="The section band beside the lyrics, with Verse and Chorus bars" />

## Make a section {#make}

Select the lines, then press <kbd>V</kbd> for a verse, <kbd>C</kbd> for a chorus, <kbd>B</kbd> for a bridge or <kbd>I</kbd> for an intro, or click a chip. Segue numbers them for you: Verse 1, Verse 2.

## Resize, split and rename {#resize}

Drag the top or bottom edge of a bar to take in more lines or fewer. Right-click a bar to rename it, split it, merge it with the next one, or unwrap it.

## Repeats on stage {#repeats}

A repeated chorus can show in full every time or once, collapsed. Press <kbd>O</kbd> on stage to switch for that song; Segue remembers it.
