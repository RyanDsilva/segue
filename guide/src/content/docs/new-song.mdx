Press <kbd>⌘N</kbd> (**File > New Song**). Segue opens a box to paste or type a song, because most songs start as a chord sheet from somewhere.

<Shot id="new-song" alt="The New Song box with Start blank under it" />

## Start blank {#blank}

Click **Start blank** to open an empty song in the editor, with the song card above it for the title, key, tempo and length. Write the song in [Text mode](/guide/text-mode), or switch to Visual to place chords over lyrics.

During a show, New Song always starts blank.
