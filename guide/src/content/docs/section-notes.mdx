Choose **Section Notes…** from the editor or the song's menu, pick a section, and type a note. To go straight to one section, right-click its band and choose **Add Note to Chorus #2…**.

<Shot id="notes-sheet" alt="The Section Notes sheet with a note on Chorus" />

## Every time, or just once {#placement}

When a section plays more than once, **Shows on** picks where the note goes: **Every time**, or one play such as **Chorus #2**. New notes start at Every time, and a second note on a section goes below the first.

A repeat written as `{chorus}` has no lines of its own, so a note there shows on stage only. If you add a section before a placed note and Segue cannot tell which one it belongs to, the note shows **Check placement**: pick its place again to confirm.

## Show on stage only {#stage-only}

Notes are written into the song file and show in the chart. Turn on **Show on stage only** to keep a note off the printed chart and see it only during a show (a PDF includes it only with [Stage notes and cues](/guide/print-and-pdf#options) on). A stage-only note stays with its section when you edit the song.
