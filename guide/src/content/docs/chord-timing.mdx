---
title: Chord timing and key changes
description: Set how long each chord lasts, push a chord early, tap the timing along with a click, and mark key, time and tempo changes in the chart.
---

How long each chord lasts drives [Play along](/guide/edit-a-song#play-along), the bass walk-ups and each song's [time in a set](/guide/running-time). ChordPro has no chord lengths, so Segue starts from the chart (bar lines, beat marks, a grid) or one bar per chord, and you correct what it got wrong. Your corrections are saved with the song, not in its ChordPro file.

## The chord-length lane {#lane}

Open **Play Along**: the lane under its controls shows each chord as long as it lasts. The edge says where the length came from:

| Edge | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Solid | From the chart: bar lines, beat marks or a grid |
| Dashed | An estimate from the song's rule |
| Solid with a dot | A length you set |
| Solid with a dot and a tick | A length you tapped |

Click a chord, then:

- type its length in **Beats**: `3`, or a part of a beat as `2.5` or `2½`
- press <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> for whole beats, or use **-** and **+**
- drag its right edge: it snaps to beats, to eighths with <kbd>⌥</kbd>, to sixteenths with <kbd>⌥⇧</kbd>
- press <kbd>⌫</kbd>, or **Use the Song's Rule**, to put it back

Each change is one undo step. Where the key, time or tempo changes, a marker above the chord says so ("Key: A", "3/4", "♩ 120"). <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click another chord, or press <kbd>⇧←</kbd> <kbd>⇧→</kbd>, to select a run of chords.

## Push a chord early {#push}

A pushed chord comes in ahead of the beat, as in an anticipation. Select it and press <kbd>&lt;</kbd> to cycle through an eighth early, a sixteenth early and on the beat, or pick from **Push**. The block starts that much early, with a notch where the beat is; the chord before it gets shorter and the bars stay where they were.

## Tap the timing {#tap}

Click **Tap Timing** to set the lengths of the range you're playing by tapping along. Pick a mode first; Segue remembers it.

- **With Click**: one bar of count-in, then Segue's click at the song's tempo (a song without a tempo taps with **No Click**). Each tap lands on the nearest beat (or half beat with **Half beats** ticked, or while you hold <kbd>⌥</kbd>). Tick **Chords** or **Bass** to hear them as they're timed now.
- **No Click**: no sound. Your first tap starts the first chord, and Segue works out the tempo from your taps.

While you tap, the pad shows the chord it's waiting for and how many taps so far.

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Page Down</kbd> (a pedal) | The next chord starts now; the tap after the last chord ends it |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Skip the next chord: it keeps its length |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Stop |

Stopping saves nothing. The lane shows each chord you changed with its old length and new one ("4 → 3"), with the old length as a bar underneath, and the chords you skipped marked. **Keep** (<kbd>Return</kbd>) saves them as your corrections in one undo step; **Discard** (<kbd>Esc</kbd>) leaves everything as it was. Without the click, the review shows the tempo from your taps: adjust it with **-** and **+**, or **½×** and **2×** if each tap was really two beats or half a beat. **Set Song Tempo** writes it into the song as `{tempo}`; nothing changes the song's tempo unless you press it.

Tap Timing works in the editor and the library, never on stage, and not during a show.

## Key changes {#key-changes}

A `{key}`, `{time}` or `{tempo}` part way through a song counts from where it's written: walk-ups, Nashville numbers, note spelling, the stage's scale line and play-along all follow it. The library, filters and the song's header keep the song's first key.

When a section's chords clearly fit another key, the editor shows a quiet chip by it: "Looks like a key change to A". **Insert `{key: A}`** writes the change before the section; **×** dismisses it for that section. Neither happens without a click, and both undo.

## Insert a key, time or tempo change {#insert}

In the editor, right-click a section's name or a line in Text mode's preview, or a section's band in Visual mode, and choose **Insert Key Change Here...**, **Insert Time Change Here...** or **Insert Tempo Change Here...**. **Song > Insert** does the same at the line with the cursor (Text mode) or the selected section (Visual mode). Pick the key, or type the time signature or BPM, and press **Insert**: one line, such as `{key: A}`, goes into the song before that point. With a capo, the key is the written key, and the popover shows the key that sounds.
