---
title: Rehearse a set
description: Run the set on stage at home, exactly like a show, without adding it to your gig log.
---
Open the setlist and choose **Setlist > Rehearse** (<kbd>⌥⌘↩</kbd>).

## How it differs from a show {#differences}

- Rehearsals never go in the [gig log](/guide/gig-log).
- The [metronome](/guide/metronome#click) can click: press <kbd>K</kbd>.
- Gig Mode Shortcuts don't run unless you turn on **Also run them for rehearsals** in **Settings > Live**.

Everything else works as on stage: the keys, requests and the set order.

## Loop a section {#loop}

Press <kbd>L</kbd> to loop the section you're in. The header shows "Looping" with the section's name, its chip on the [section map](/guide/go-on-stage#section-map) is ringed in coral, and a dashed line marks it in the song. When you reach the end of the section, the song goes back to its top and waits one bar while the [metronome](/guide/metronome#count-in) counts you in, then carries on. With auto-scroll on it wraps by itself; without it, every <kbd>Page Down</kbd> that would leave the section wraps instead. Press <kbd>L</kbd> again to stop, or jump to another section or move to another song.

## Rehearse one song {#one-song}

To run through a single song, select it in your library and click **Rehearse** in its header, or choose **Library > Rehearse Song** (<kbd>⌥⌘↩</kbd>). **Live** (<kbd>⇧⌘↩</kbd>) plays it on the stage as at a show. Either way the song plays in full as written, there's no pre-show checklist, and nothing goes in your gig log or play counts. Press <kbd>⌘.</kbd> to end it and come back to the library.
