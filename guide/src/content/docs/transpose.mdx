## Change the key for this view {#transpose}

Transpose and Capo change what you see, not the file. To write the new key into the song itself, choose **Apply to Source**: Segue rewrites the chords and the key and keeps the capo.

## Shapes or concert pitch {#capo}

With a capo, **Shapes** shows the chords as you play them and **Concert** shows the pitch that sounds, for keys and voice.

## Nashville numbers {#nashville}

Turn on **Nashville** to see each chord as a number in the song's key.

## Guitar and piano diagrams {#diagrams}

Turn on **Diagrams** and choose Guitar, with a tuning, or Piano. Shapes you draw yourself are saved in the song as a ChordPro `{define}`, so they travel with the file.
