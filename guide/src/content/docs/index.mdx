## Your first show in five steps

<Steps>
1. **Pick a library folder.** Segue asks the first time it opens. Your songs live there as plain ChordPro files.
2. **Try the Welcome library.** Four songs and a Welcome Set, written for Segue. Delete them whenever you like.
3. **Bring a song in.** Copy a chord sheet from any site and press <kbd>⌥⌘V</kbd>. Check the review, then import it.
4. **Build a set.** Press <kbd>⇧⌘N</kbd> for a new setlist, then <kbd>Space</kbd> ticks songs in the order you want them.
5. **Go on stage.** <kbd>⌘↩</kbd> starts the show. <kbd>→</kbd> is the next song, and <kbd>?</kbd> shows every key.
</Steps>

## Deleted something?

Deleted songs, setlists and gigs go to the Trash, and Edit > Undo brings them back. See [Back up and restore](/guide/back-up#trash).
