<Steps>
1. Open **Settings > Instrument** and turn on **I play bass**.
2. Pick **4** or **5** strings and your **Tuning**.
3. Open any song or go on stage: each chord now shows the bass note to play.
</Steps>

## Bass notes {#bass-notes}

In the bass-note view each chord shows the note to play: the slash bass if there is one, else the root, so `G/B` shows B. You don't need to be a bassist to use it: a keyboard player covering bass can turn it on too.

- In the editor, click **Bass** in the bar above the song. The visual editor keeps the chord pills, so the notes show in the preview and on stage.
- On stage, press <kbd>B</kbd> to switch between bass notes and chords.

Both switch every song at once, and so does **Show bass notes instead of chords** in **Settings > Instrument**.

## Sounding pitch {#sounding-pitch}

Bass notes are always the pitch that sounds, whatever capo the guitar chart uses, so a chart in G shapes with capo 2 shows A. A key or capo you set for a setlist slot is included too. **Shapes** and **Concert** are greyed out while bass notes show.

## Nashville numbers and the full chord {#nashville}

With the song's **Nashville** switch on, each note carries its number, like "5 · D". Turn it off and you see "D". Turn on **Show the full chord beside the note** to see the chord small beside it.

On stage the header also shows the key's scale, like "Sounding A major: A B C# D E F# G#".

## The mini neck on stage {#mini-neck}

With **Mini neck on stage** on, a small neck at the bottom left of the stage shows the chord you're reading. Every note of the chord is a dot labelled by its degree (R, 3, 5, b7 and so on), and the note to play is the strongest one. When a walk-up is coming, its notes show as dashed rings numbered 1, 2, 3 in the order you play them. The neck follows **Strings** and **Tuning** in Settings, and never takes a click.

## The fretboard in the editor {#fretboard}

With **I play bass** and **Fretboard in the editor** on, the strip under the song becomes a bass fretboard, one cell per chord: every chord tone by degree, with the note to play filled in. While you play bass, the bar above the song shows **Fretboard** in place of Diagrams, and a "Bass, BEADG" menu with **Fills as Tab** and **Instrument Settings…** to change strings or tuning. The song's own guitar or piano setting is kept for when you turn it off.

## Walk-ups {#walk-ups}

A walk-up is a short run of notes after a chord that leads into the next one, like `G A B ›` then `C`. A note outside the key is marked "°", as in `A B C#° ›`.

Turn them on in **Settings > Instrument** with **Walk-ups on charts**: **Into sections** for just the changes into a new section, or **Every change**. They show only in the bass-note view.

### Pick a different walk-up {#pick}

In the editor preview or a library song's preview, click a walk-up to see the other options, best first, or **Nothing here** to leave that change plain. Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move and <kbd>Return</kbd> to choose. **Reset to suggestion** goes back to Segue's pick.

### Hear the walk-ups {#hear-walk-ups}

Each option has a play button: it plays the change at the song's tempo (90 if the song has none), two bars, with the walk-up on the last beats. <kbd>Space</kbd> plays or stops the highlighted option and <kbd>A</kbd> to <kbd>D</kbd> highlight one. Turn on **Loop** to hear the change over and over: moving to another option switches to it at the start of the next pass, so you can compare them back to back. **Use this** or <kbd>Return</kbd> keeps the highlighted option; playing never saves anything. In the Walk-ups list, the play button beside each change plays the walk-up the chart shows.

In the editor, click a bass fretboard cell to hear its note; <kbd>⌥</kbd>-click plays the chord with it.

Your picks are saved in the song's small `.segue.json` file, never in its `.cho` file. The stage shows your pick, else the top suggestion, and never offers options or plays a sound.

### The Walk-ups list {#walk-ups-list}

Choose **Walk-ups…** in the editor's top bar, or right-click a song in the library, to see every change in the song, grouped by section. Click a row to pick for that change.

At the top, **Feel** sets the style for this song: **Default**, **Pop**, **Rock**, **Worship**, **Country / gospel** or **Bollywood**. The feel only changes which walk-ups come first. Default uses **Default feel** from Settings.

If you edit the song and some saved walk-ups no longer match, the list's footer shows them with a **Remove** button each, and **Remove All**.

## Fills on charts and on stage {#fills-on-stage}

A fill you saved shows in the bass-note view as note names after each chord it plays over, like `G A` after G and `D E ›` after C, then the chord it lands on. It takes the place of the walk-up on those changes, and follows **Walk-ups on charts**: with walk-ups off, fills are hidden too. A fill set to show on one play, or **Last time** (the last time that section plays in the whole song), shows only there.

A fill whose **Show as** is **Tab** draws a small tab under the line holding its first chord instead: one line per string, frets spaced by rhythm, bar lines and the chords above. Press <kbd>T</kbd> on stage to show every fill as tab, and again for names. It's remembered, like <kbd>B</kbd>, and you can set it with **Fills as Tab** in the editor's "Bass, BEADG" menu or **Show fills as** in Settings. In a song with no fills, <kbd>T</kbd> just says so.

While the chord you're reading is part of a fill, the mini neck shows the fill's notes over that chord as dashed rings, numbered across the whole fill (3, 4, 5 over the second chord). Fills never make a sound on stage, in Live or Rehearsal, except through Rehearsal play-along.

## Settings > Instrument {#settings}

| Setting | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Show bass notes instead of chords | The bass-note view, for every song and the stage |
| Show the full chord beside the note | The chord small beside each note |
| Mini neck on stage | The neck at the bottom left of the stage |
| I play bass | Your bass profile; also turns bass notes on |
| Strings, Tuning | 4 or 5 strings, a preset or **Custom…** (type "BEADG") |
| Fretboard in the editor | A bass fretboard under the song |
| Walk-ups on charts | Off, Into sections or Every change |
| Default feel | The feel for songs that have none of their own |
| Show fills as | Each fill's choice, or Always tab (<kbd>T</kbd> on stage) |
