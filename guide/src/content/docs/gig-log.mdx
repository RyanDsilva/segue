Choose **Gig Log** in the sidebar. Each show lists the venue, the date and time, and every song: played, skipped or requested.

<Shot id="gig-log" alt="A gig in the gig log with played, skipped and requested songs" />

## Save the order you played {#order}

If you changed the order during the show, Segue asks afterwards whether to save the played order to the setlist. **Save Show Order** updates the setlist; **Discard** keeps it as it was.

## Add the details {#edit}

Add the venue, a rating and notes to any gig. To remove a gig, choose **Move Gig to Trash**.
