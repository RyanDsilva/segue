<Steps>
1. On stage, press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> or <kbd>/</kbd> and type a few letters of a title or a line you remember. Spellings vary, so "pyar" finds "Pyaar".
2. Press <kbd>Return</kbd> to play it now, <kbd>⌥↩</kbd> to play it next, or <kbd>⌘↩</kbd> to add it to your requests, with an optional note such as "table 4".
</Steps>

<Shot id="stage-search" alt="Quick search on stage, finding Kitaabon Wali Gali" />

## The request queue {#queue}

Press <kbd>Q</kbd> to see your requests and <kbd>R</kbd> to play the oldest one.

## No repeats {#no-repeats}

A song plays once per show. If it was already played tonight, Segue says so. If it is still coming up in the set, Segue offers **Move to Next** instead. A song you skipped can be added back.
