## Export a library archive {#export}

Choose **File > Export Library Archive…** (also in **Settings > Data**). Segue saves one zip of your songs, setlists, shows and filters, as they are in the library folder. Keep it somewhere safe, or use it to move to another Mac.

Your library folder is also covered by Time Machine and any other backup tool.

## Deleted something? {#trash}

Deleted songs, setlists, smart filters and gigs go to the Trash. Choose **Edit > Undo** straight away, or drag the file back from the Trash into your library folder. Segue asks first only when a song is used in a setlist.

## Reset settings {#reset}

**Settings > Data > Reset All Settings…** puts every setting back to its default. It keeps your library, your Privacy choices and any downloaded model.
