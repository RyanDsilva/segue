The song card sits above the song in the library and in the editor. Click any field to change it. Segue writes it into the song's ChordPro file, or into the small details file beside it for tags and proficiency.

## Artists and Movie / Album {#artists}

A song can have several artists. In the library, the artists and the movie or album sit under the title: double-click a line to edit it in place. In the editor, the card's first two tokens open the same fields.

Artists are chips. Type a name and press <kbd>Return</kbd> or <kbd>,</kbd> to add it, exactly as you typed it. As you type, Segue suggests artists already in your library; press <kbd>↓</kbd> then <kbd>Return</kbd>, or <kbd>Tab</kbd>, to take one. Drag a chip to reorder it; the first is the main artist. In an empty field, <kbd>⌫</kbd> twice removes the last chip. A chip like "Vishal & Shekhar" offers **Split** to make it two artists. Each artist is its own standard `{artist}` line in the ChordPro file.

**Movie / Album** is one name, saved as `{album}`. Search finds songs by it, and smart filters and the set assistant use it ("from Kalank").

## Set essentials {#essentials}

Key and tempo are the **set essentials**: Segue uses them to time a set and plan its flow. A small dot marks each one that is missing, on the card and on the song's row in the library. The **Missing essentials** filter in the sidebar lists every song that needs one.

## Key {#key}

Pick the key and Major or Minor. If the song has no key yet, Segue suggests one from its chords. With a capo, the key is the key of the shapes you play.

## Tempo {#tempo}

Type the beats per minute, or tap along with the song: each tap updates the tempo. The tempo also sets the song's time in a set: Segue counts the bars you play at that tempo (see [Running time](/guide/running-time)). A song with no tempo has no time, and set totals show it as unknown.

## Capo, time signature, proficiency and tags {#more}

**Add capo or time signature** shows those two fields. Proficiency is Learning, Ready or Pro, told apart by fill as well as colour. Tags are free words like "opener" or "ballad"; see [Tags and smart filters](/guide/smart-filters).
