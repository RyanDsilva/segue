title: Singer view
description: Lyrics only, with every chord hidden, for the singer. Press V on stage, put a Singer Screen on a second display, or preview a song as the singer will see it.
---

Singer view draws a song with no chords in the way: bigger lyrics, the section names and the [section map](/guide/go-on-stage#section-map) so the singer knows where the band is, and the chart notes and stage cues where they belong. It is only a way of drawing the song. Your song, its settings and your setlists never change.

## What it hides and keeps {#hides}

Hidden: chords and chord rows, Nashville numbers, capo shapes, walk-ups, fills, tab and grid blocks, chord diagrams, the bass mini neck, and the song and slot notes (they are usually reminders for players).

Kept: section names, the section map, the "2 of 3" badges and the CHORDS and 1ST LINE tags, `{comment}` lines such as "All together", [stage cues](/guide/section-notes), the [metronome](/guide/metronome) and the next song's first line. A section with no words, like an intro or a solo, shows its name and "Instrumental". A word with a chord in the middle reads whole: "Tu[G]jh" shows as "Tujh". Romanized Hindi and Devanagari lyrics are drawn exactly as you wrote them.

The header shows the key the band sounds in after the set's transpose and capo, like "Key A, 96", never a capo shape.

## On stage {#stage}

Press <kbd>V</kbd> to switch to singer view and back, in a show or a rehearsal, even mid-song: the stage stays on the same section. The header shows a "Singer" chip while it's on. You can also use **Live > Singer View** (<kbd>⇧⌘V</kbd>) or **Settings > Live > Show lyrics only on stage (Singer view)**. Segue remembers the choice for your next show, and the [pre-show checklist](/guide/go-on-stage#checklist) reminds you when it's on, with a **Show Chords** button.

- Lyrics are larger than in the chart and never smaller than 28 pt. A line is never broken in two: when the song would only fit by wrapping lines, it shows in one large column that scrolls instead.
- <kbd>⌘+</kbd> and <kbd>⌘-</kbd> zoom singer view for every song, without changing a song's own zoom.
- [Arranged](/guide/arrange-sections) sections shown as chords only show their first line instead, still tagged CHORDS, so the singer sees the section is played.
- <kbd>B</kbd> and <kbd>T</kbd> do nothing while singer view is on: your bass view comes back as it was when you press <kbd>V</kbd> again.
- Jumping, looping, auto-scroll and [play along](/guide/rehearse#play-along) work exactly as with chords.

## A Singer Screen on a second display {#singer-screen}

With a second display connected and a show or rehearsal on stage, choose **Window > Singer Screen**. The singer's display shows the song in singer view, with the stage's header and section map, and follows you: the same song, the same section and the same place in it, as you scroll, jump or loop. Your own stage keeps its chords. With three or more displays, choose which one from the submenu; Segue remembers it, and the pre-show checklist warns when that display isn't connected. The Singer Screen closes when the show ends or its display is unplugged.

## Preview a song for a singer {#preview}

In the library, the **Chords / Singer** control above a song's chart shows it as a singer will see it. The song editor has the same control at the end of its display controls: in Text mode the preview shows lyrics only while you type, and in Visual mode the chart becomes a read-only page (turn Singer off to edit chords). **Song > Singer Preview** (<kbd>⇧⌘Y</kbd>) does the same. This preview has its own switch, so it never turns the stage into singer view.

## Printing lyric sheets {#print}

Lyric sheets print through PDF export's **Lyrics** chart, with the same rules: no chords, instrumental sections named, and the sounding key in the header.
