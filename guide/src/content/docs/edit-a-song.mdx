## Visual and Text {#modes}

The editor has two modes. **Visual** shows the lyrics with chords above them. **Text** edits the ChordPro source directly; see [Text mode, the slash menu and ChordPro](/guide/text-mode).

<Shot id="editor-visual" alt="The editor in Visual mode with chord pills over the lyrics" />

## Place and move chords {#chords}

In Visual mode, click above a syllable to add a chord. Drag a chord pill to move it to another syllable, or select it and use the arrow keys to nudge it one syllable at a time.

## Saving {#autosave}

There is nothing to save: every change is written as you type, and the editor shows "Saved". <kbd>⌘S</kbd> just confirms "All changes saved". **Done** closes the editor. Edit > Undo works across your changes.

If the file changes in another app while you edit, Segue asks whether to reload it, overwrite it with your edits, or save your edits as a new song.

## Playing bass {#bass}

Click **Bass** in the bar above the song to see the note to play instead of each chord, at sounding pitch, in the preview. With walk-ups on, click one in the preview to pick another, or choose **Walk-ups…** for the whole song. See [Playing bass](/guide/play-bass).

## Hear a chord {#hear}

Click a chord in the preview, or in a library song's preview, to hear it with its bass note underneath, at the pitch the band will sound (capo and transpose included). In Visual mode, <kbd>⌥</kbd>-click a chord pill to hear it; a plain click still selects it. <kbd>⌥</kbd>-click a diagram in the strip under the song to hear that chord.

Choose the sound in **Settings > Audio**:

| Setting | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Chord sound | Piano or clean guitar |
| Chords play as | Strum (low to high) or block |
| Bass | Finger or pick bass |
| Volume | How loud sound plays |
| Test | Plays a G chord with its bass note |

Sound plays through your Mac's sound output. It never plays during a show: chord clicks and play buttons are greyed out while a show is on stage.

## Play along {#play-along}

Click **Play Along** in the toolbar (or choose **Song > Play Along**, <kbd>⇧⌘P</kbd>) to open the bar under the song; in the library it's above a song's chart. Press **Play** (<kbd>⌥Space</kbd>) to hear bass, chords and click at the song's tempo after one bar of count-in. The chord at the playhead is underlined in the preview, which scrolls to keep it in view.

| Control | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Range | The whole song, one section, or a section to the end |
| Loop | Repeats the range; on by default for a section |
| Bass, Chords, Click, Walk-ups | Turn each part on or off; Walk-ups starts as your charts show them |
| Tempo | Slower or faster for practice, or 50%, 75% or 100% of the song's tempo; never saved to the song |

A song without a tempo plays at 90.

ChordPro has no chord lengths, so Segue estimates them: from bar lines and beat marks in the chart, else one bar per chord. The strip under the controls shows each chord as long as it lasts: a solid edge comes from the chart, a dashed edge is an estimate, a dot is a length you set, and a dot with a tick is one you tapped. To change one, click it, then use **Beats** (or type 1-9, or drag its right edge); <kbd>⌫</kbd> or **Use the Song's Rule** puts it back. Parts of a beat, pushes and **Tap Timing** are in [Chord timing and key changes](/guide/chord-timing). **Timing** sets the rule for the whole song (a chord per half bar, bar or two bars, each line lasting 1, 2 or 4 bars, or every chord a set number of beats) and resets your changes. Changes apply from the next bar while it plays, and they're saved with the song, not in its ChordPro file.

## Spell-check and autocorrect {#spelling}

Spell-check, autocorrect, smart quotes and text replacement are off in the editor. Lyrics are often in other languages or in romanized Hindi with casual spellings, and a chart has to stay exactly as you typed it.
