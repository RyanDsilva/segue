Segue Help

Segue keeps your songs, charts and setlists as plain files in a folder you choose, and runs your show from a dark, full-screen stage view. New here? Start with the Welcome library.

Getting started

The first time you open Segue, choose a folder for your library. Segue keeps every song, setlist and gig log there as ordinary files. You can switch to another folder later with Library > Choose Library Folder.

Deleted something by mistake?

See Deleting songs, setlists and gigs.
