  • Never too small.

    Set a minimum lyric size. If a song won't fit, Segue scrolls instead of shrinking it.

  • Know what's next.

    The footer shows the next song with its key and capo, or the request you queued.

  • Take requests.

    Press Cmd-K, type a title or a line you remember, and play it now, next or later.

  • One pedal, whole set.

    Page Down scrolls, then moves on to the next song. A page-turner pedal just works.

  • Back where you were.

    If your Mac restarts mid-show, Segue offers to pick up right where you left off.

  • A quiet Mac.

    Run your own Shortcut as the show starts, for example to turn on a Focus.

The details, done properly.

  • Your songs, as files.

    One ChordPro file per song, in a folder you choose. No account, no lock-in.

  • Search the way you spell.

    Find any half-remembered line, romanized Hindi included.

  • Guitar and piano.

    Chord diagrams for both, with your own guitar shapes saved in the song.

  • Learning, Ready, Pro.

    Proficiency marks and tags show what's ready for the gig.

  • Nothing to save.

    Edits save as you go. Deleted songs go to the Trash.

  • At home in Finder.

    Open .cho files from Finder, preview them in Quick Look, and export your whole library.

  • Rehearse first.

    Run the set at home to time each song, without a gig log entry.

  • Keyboard first.

    Stage mode runs from the keyboard. Press ? to see every key.

Privacy

Your songs stay yours.

  • No account.

    No sign-in. Your songs, setlists and gig history are files on your Mac.

  • Never uploaded.

    Segue never uploads your songs. A bug report goes out only when you press Send.

  • Your choice.

    Usage statistics and crash reports are two separate questions, with nothing picked for you.
