Stage mode

Stage mode is dark, full screen, and built to be read from a stool.

Starting a show

Before a show starts, Segue shows a short checklist, Before you start: whether the charger is connected, a reminder to turn the screen brightness up, and whether your Focus is on. You can hide a row for this show or for good.

Moving through the set

Right Arrow or Space goes to the next song and Left Arrow to the previous one. Down Arrow or Page Down and Up Arrow or Page Up scroll within the song, and Home goes back to the top. A page-turner pedal that sends these keys works without any setup.

Auto-fit and the minimum lyric size

Segue fits each song to the screen. Set the smallest size you can read in Settings > Live, under Minimum lyric size. Songs that don't fit at that size scroll instead of shrinking.

Auto-scroll

Press A to start or pause auto-scroll, [ to slow it down and ] to speed it up.

Requests and quick search

Press Cmd-K or / and type a few letters of a title or a line you remember. Then press Return to play it now, Option-Return to play it next, or Cmd-Return to add it to your requests, with an optional note such as "table 4". Press Q to see your requests and R to play the oldest one.

Skipping and reordering

Press X to skip a song and S to see the set order. After the show, if the order changed, Segue asks whether to save the played order to the setlist.

If Segue quits mid-show

If your Mac restarts or Segue quits during a show, Segue offers to resume it the next time it opens, at the same song and scroll position.

Ending a show

Press Cmd-. to end the show, then Return to confirm or Esc to keep playing. The set is saved to your gig log.

Zoom

Cmd-+ zooms in, Cmd-- zooms out and Cmd-0 returns to the actual size.
