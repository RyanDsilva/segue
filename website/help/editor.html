Editing songs

Edit a chart as text, or place chords right on the words.

Text and Visual modes

The editor has two modes. Text edits the ChordPro source directly. Visual shows the lyrics with chords above them, so you can place each chord where it belongs.

ChordPro basics

ChordPro is plain text. Chords go in square brackets right before the syllable they belong to, and directives in curly braces set things like the title, key and sections:

{title: Lanterns}
{key: G}

{start_of_verse}
[G]Lanterns on the [D]water, the [Em]boats come [C]in
[G]Ropes along the [D]railing, the [C]night begins
{end_of_verse}

Placing chords on syllables

In Visual mode, put each chord over the syllable where it changes. Segue writes the ChordPro for you.

Transpose, capo and Nashville numbers

The editor's top bar has Transpose, Capo (0 to 11) and Nashville. With a capo set, choose Shapes to see the chord shapes you play, or Concert to see the pitch that sounds.

Guitar and piano diagrams

Turn on Diagrams to show chord diagrams, and choose Guitar or Piano, with a tuning for guitar. Shapes you draw yourself are saved in the song as a ChordPro {define}, so they travel with the file.

Saving

Spell-check and autocorrect

Spell-check, autocorrect, smart quotes and text replacement are off in the editor. Lyrics are often in other languages or in romanized Hindi with casual spellings, and a chart has to stay exactly as you typed it.
