Press kit

Segue is a free Mac app for performing musicians: lyrics, chords and setlists, with a stage view that fits every song on one screen.

About Segue

Segue keeps your songs as plain ChordPro files in a folder you choose, lets you edit charts as text or by placing chords on syllables, builds setlists with per-show key and capo, and runs the gig from a dark, full-screen stage view that never shrinks lyrics below your minimum size. It handles requests mid-set, picks up where you left off if anything goes wrong, and has no account, no ads and no subscription. Search understands romanized Hindi with casual spellings.
